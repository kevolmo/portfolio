Beyond the dashboards

A career built at the intersection of people, process, and data.

My path is not a straight line—and that is one of its strengths. Business strategy, operational leadership, and business intelligence each taught me a different way to understand systems and improve how people work within them.

“I do more than report what happened. I connect the data to the process, the people, and the next decision.”
Based inOrlando, Florida
Professional directionBI · Analytics · Process optimization
My through-line

I make complex work understandable, measurable, and easier to improve.

My journey

Four chapters.
One evolving practice.

Each chapter added a new lens. Together, they shape how I diagnose problems, communicate evidence, lead change, and build systems people can sustain.

01Business foundation

Bachelor of Science

Learning to connect people, value, and business outcomes.

My undergraduate studies built a durable business foundation: communicate clearly, understand stakeholder value, connect work to commercial outcomes, and shape information for the people making decisions. Those lessons now guide how I present data and design tools for adoption.

Stakeholders · Value · Communication
02Operational leadership

High-volume operations

Learning how systems behave when the pressure is real.

Frontline and management roles taught me to balance people, workload, quality, safety, and customer commitments in real time. At Amazon, that meant controlling work in process across 15-minute intervals. In my current environment, it means building a cross-functional team that understands the entire flow and can redirect itself to business need.

People · Flow · Accountability
03Analytical discipline

M.S. · Business Intelligence

Turning operational questions into evidence leaders can use.

Graduate study strengthened my ability to move from a business question to research, data gathering, modeling, visualization, governance, forecasting, and decision communication. The Google Data Analytics Professional Certificate reinforced a practical, structured approach to analysis.

Evidence · Models · Decisions
04Transformation practice

BI + process analytics

Building the system behind the dashboard.

Today I combine all three foundations. I build Power BI reporting, SAP automation, Power Platform applications, SharePoint operating systems, and process controls—but the technology is only part of the work. The real outcome is shared understanding, easier execution, stronger decisions, and a team that can sustain the improvement.

Insight · Workflow · Adoption

How the journey shaped my work

Principles I carry into every problem.

01

Start with the business question

A dashboard is useful only when it makes a real decision clearer.

02

Build close to the work

The people executing the process reveal where the data and workflow actually break.

03

Design for trust

Accuracy, transparency, usability, and shared definitions determine whether a tool is adopted.

04

Leave capability behind

The best solution makes the team more independent, not more dependent on its creator.
